# 05.10 Requirements & Lifecycle: Teaching 2 (about 8 min)

Source: Developer Module 5, screen 10 of 25. Title: "Systems lifecycle for Claude applications".

The requirements from 05.08 are phase one of a longer arc. Naming it as the systems lifecycle places the rest of the module's deployment, versioning and boundary work in the right phase instead of leaving it as unrelated tasks.

## The seven phases, applied to a Claude application
1. **Requirements:** capture functional and infrastructure needs.
2. **Design:** choose the platform, the model and the trust boundaries.
3. **Build:** write the agent, tools and prompts.
4. **Test:** evals, unit, integration and end-to-end checks.
5. **Deploy:** pin the version; gate promotion on the eval.
6. **Operate:** instrument cost, latency and errors; enforce guardrails.
7. **Iterate:** feed production findings back into requirements.

The phases are those earlier modules taught one at a time; seeing them as a lifecycle shows how they connect.

## Gates between phases
A gate is a decision to move to the next phase, and is where a regulated engagement keeps control. Examples: don't go from design to build until the platform satisfies the residency requirement; don't go from deploy to full production until the new version clears the eval against the pinned baseline. Putting work in the right phase and refusing to skip a gate keeps a Claude application reviewable.

- **Handles well:** placing each piece of engineering work in its phase, with a defined artifact and gate.
- **Adds cost:** gates add checkpoints that a team under deadline is tempted to skip.
- **Use something else:** a one-off experiment may collapse phases; a regulated deployment cannot.
