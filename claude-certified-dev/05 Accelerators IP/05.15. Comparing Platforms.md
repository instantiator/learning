# 05.15 Comparing Platforms: Teaching (about 12 min)

Source: Developer Module 5, screen 15 of 25. Title: "Comparing platforms on latency, compliance, and cost so the choice survives review".

"Right for their cloud" (05.12) isn't yet an argument procurement and security will sign off on. Compare on three dimensions.

## Latency: measure from the customer's region
- A platform running in the customer's own cloud region can cut round-trip time versus a farther first-party endpoint. Trade-off: the first-party API typically gets new capabilities before other platforms.
- The number is only valid when measured from the customer's actual region with their actual payload. Measuring from your laptop hides the round-trip penalty that appears once the workload runs where the customer is.
- Within Bedrock, global vs regional endpoints is also the primary residency control and can affect cost; measure from the customer's region against both before committing.

## Compliance often ends the debate
A customer already certified on one cloud is unlikely to re-certify on another. **Data residency** = a rule that data must be processed in a specific country or region. Certifications and who can audit access differ by platform; regulated finance and healthcare customers treat these as pass-or-fail, not trade-offs.
- The first-party Claude API may not offer EU data residency (confirm current regional coverage at platform.claude.com); EU-only residency typically needs Bedrock or Vertex AI.
- On Microsoft Foundry, hosting is per model: Azure-hosted models run inference end-to-end on Azure; Anthropic-hosted Foundry models do not satisfy EU regional residency. Confirm per model and deployment with Microsoft.
- Raise the compliance constraint during scoping, or it surfaces at contract review after the work is done.

## Cost beyond the per-token rate
Per-token rates are broadly aligned across platforms; total cost moves on egress, platform fees and integration effort. A lower token price can cost more in total once data transfer and integration are counted. Instrument cost per call per platform; confirm current pricing pages at scoping.

## Cross-platform comparison reference
| Dimension | How it differs | How to measure | Where each wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| Latency | In-region platform shortens the round trip; first-party may reach new features first | From the customer's actual region with their actual payload | In-region cloud platform wins on round trip; first-party wins on earliest feature access |
| Compliance | Residency, certifications and audit controls are set by the deployment platform | Against the customer's existing certification and residency requirements during scoping | The cloud the customer already certified wins (no re-certification) |
| Cost | Token price, egress, platform fees and integration effort all vary | Total cost per call per platform, including egress and integration, not token price alone | Lowest total cost for the actual workload wins, not always the cheapest token |

- **Handles well:** measuring all three per platform turns a placement into one procurement will sign off.
- **Adds cost:** instrumenting latency, compliance and cost across platforms is real measurement work before code ships.
- **Use something else:** if the customer's compliance requirement is already pass-or-fail, skip the full comparison; it decides the placement alone.
