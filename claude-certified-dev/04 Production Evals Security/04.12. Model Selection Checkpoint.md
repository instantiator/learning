# 04.12 Model Selection: Checkpoint (about 2 min)

Source: Developer Module 4, screen 12 of 23. Title: "Choose the model and name the deciding constraint". A multiple-choice exercise, not submitted; the answers below are the ones 04.11 supports.

For each scenario, pick the model tier and the one constraint driving the decision.

| Scenario | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A high-volume classification step labels millions of short messages a day; an eval shows Haiku holding the quality bar | **Haiku**, deciding constraint: **cost at volume**, since the eval confirms the quality bar still holds | The eval is what licenses stepping down; at this volume the cost saving dominates. Opus or Sonnet would pay for capability the task doesn't need. |
| A multi-step agent plans a dependent refactor where a wrong early step is expensive; an eval shows Sonnet missing the bar on the hardest cases | **Opus**, deciding constraint: **quality on hard reasoning where the cost of a wrong answer is high** | The measured miss on hard cases plus costly mistakes is the step-up condition. |
| Mixed traffic: most requests simple lookups, a few complex synthesis | **Route:** a Sonnet (or Haiku) default with an **Opus override** on complex requests, deciding constraint: **the traffic is mixed** | Pay for the larger model only where needed; one model for everything either overpays (Opus) or under-delivers (Haiku) on part of the traffic. |

Pattern (04.11): start at Sonnet, move up or down only on a measured eval result, and route when traffic is mixed.
