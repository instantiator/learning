# 01.01 Orientation (about 2 min)

Source: Developer Module 1, MSO Foundations, screen 1 of 9.

Module 1 covers the vocabulary and technical basics that the rest of the Developer course assumes you already know.

## Learning goals
1. **LLM mechanics:** what a token is; the context window as a fixed budget; why sampling makes outputs vary; what non-determinism means for testing and evals.
2. **Model choice vs reasoning:** the Claude model family and its capability tiers; picking a model is a separate decision from switching on a reasoning mode such as extended thinking.
3. **Few-shot prompting:** zero-shot vs one-shot vs multi-shot, and the cost/quality trade-off of adding examples.
4. **Access patterns:** SDK vs raw REST; synchronous vs streaming responses; asynchronous patterns for high-volume work.

## Module shape
9 screens, 6 sections, about 59 minutes, 2 checkpoints (the quiz and the exercise).
